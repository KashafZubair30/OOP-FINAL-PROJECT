-------------- ABOUT THE GAME --------------
A wealthy business owner hosted a party at their cottage.
During the party, a locker hidden behind a bookshelf in
the Study Room was robbed. Eight people were present and
one of them is the CULPRIT. The owner's PA has hired you
as a private detective to investigate the case.

-------------- YOUR MISSION --------------
1. Visit seven locations. Each gives a letter and a position.
2. Interview eight people. Each gives a letter and a position.
3. Letters are placed on the Scrabble board automatically.
4. When a row is full, form the word.
5. Both words together point to the culprit's role.

Good luck, Detective.
